About

DisinfoX is a Theat Intelligence Exchange Platform specialized in disinformation campaigns and fake news. It is a platform that allows to spead intelligence about disinformation campaigns and fake news in real-time to allow organizations with CTI capabilities to act on it. The platform is designed to be user-friendly for non-technical users like journalists, fact-checkers, and researchers to upload their findings and share it with the community. Additionally, the platform is designed to be STIX-friendly for technical users like analysts, threat intelligence analysts, and data scientists to use the API to access the data and integrate it into their CTI platforms.

Features

  • Upload disinformation campaigns and fake news
  • Share intelligence with the community
  • Access the data via API
  • Integrate the data into your CTI platform
